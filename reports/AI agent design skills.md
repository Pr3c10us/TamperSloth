# Agent design skills outrun their evidence

Public AI design guidance agrees on a process and disagrees on most specifics, and **no source shows that any of it reliably improves output**. The popular skills converge on one loop: ground in the subject, commit to a written direction, build, then critique the rendered result, with a rotating list of banned tells on top. The only per-round comparison found in any repo is unflattering: **taste-skill's round score never beat its no-skill baseline across five model-judged rounds (four losses, one tie)**. Measured studies size the problem, including originality of 0.592 for models against 0.764 for humans, but they test almost no fixes. The best-supported claim is a negative one that vendors concede: bans push a model to its next default rather than toward a design. Positive evidence points to decisions sourced outside the model, such as a reference, a token set or a direction picked by someone else, and it rests on anecdote, one vendor's campaign and human-subject research. For TamperSloth, the portable parts are the redesign discipline (baseline, invariants, chosen depth, diff) and the accessibility floor, while advice on stacks, packages, palettes, heroes and copy breaks or reverses, because the site already has an identity and keeps running. **No independent evidence shows that any AI live-page restyler works.** This report stops at findings and open questions, since the playbook waits on the user's own process.

**How to read the evidence.** Unlabelled claims with a link were read at that source by the research team, as of 2026-11-02. Links tagged *vendor*, *unofficial copy* or *search summary* carry that weakness: unofficial copies are prompts collected in public repos, with authenticity unconfirmed by any vendor, and search-summary items were not read in full. *Researcher test* marks an experiment run for these notes. *Repo check* marks something confirmed against the TamperSloth working tree for this report, including uncommitted files. Text led by **Inference** is reasoning, not sourced fact.

**Coverage limits.** There is no Reddit evidence: web search refused the domain, the browser refused it under a safety restriction, and nobody tried a workaround. Forum coverage therefore means Hacker News (HN), with comments paraphrased by an automated step. Google's AI Studio and Stitch guides rendered empty and went unread. Most of the largest packs were not read in full (MengTo's 179 skills, Owl-Listener's 111, plugin87's, Emil's other 15). The paid Mobbin and Refero servers were not exercised. Legal questions were not researched, and nothing here is legal advice.

## The top skills share one loop and split on retrieval versus judgment

Stars and installs signal adoption, not quality, and they disagree with each other. The most-installed design skill on [skills.sh](https://skills.sh/), `design-mobile-apps` at 1.2M, comes from a 25-star repo, [shows a failed Snyk audit](https://skills.sh/designed-by-ai/skills/design-mobile-apps) and [is a client for a commercial API](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/designed-by-ai/skills/main/skills/design-mobile-apps/SKILL.md) rather than design guidance. Skills.sh was read through a summarizer and hides some rows. The table covers design skills whose instruction text was read at source, in star order.

([frontend-design](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anthropics/skills/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md)).

Licences and runtime behavior matter for any reuse. ui-ux-pro-max, taste-skill, Hallmark and Emil's pack are MIT, Impeccable is Apache 2.0 with [a NOTICE crediting another project](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/main/NOTICE.md), and frontend-design carries [Apache 2.0 in its own folder](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/blob/main/skills/frontend-design/LICENSE.txt). [vercel-labs/agent-skills](https://github.com/vercel-labs/agent-skills) has no root licence file, though the [guidelines repo it fetches](https://github.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines) is MIT. Vercel's skill and [Impeccable's new-design runs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pbakaus/impeccable/main/README.md) fetch remote content at run time, and Impeccable's hooks can download its engine outside model approval.

**Inference:** The real divide is philosophical. ui-ux-pro-max retrieves what suits a category, while Anthropic, Impeccable, taste-skill and Hallmark treat category defaults as the problem. Agreement on 4.5:1 contrast, 44px targets, 16px body text and reduced motion says little about design insight, because those numbers come largely from WCAG and platform guidelines. The tool-heavy packs convert taste rules into checks an agent cannot argue with, but the rules remain opinions, as [taste-skill](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/main/skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md) and [avoid-ai-design](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/funboy322/avoid-ai-design/main/SKILL.md) both state.

### Builder prompts favor first impressions over interaction states

Prompt copies span April 2025 to October 2026, and [neither](https://github.com/x1xhlol/system-prompts-and-models-of-ai-tools) main [collection](https://github.com/asgeirtj/system_prompts_leaks) verifies authenticity. The [v0 copy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/x1xhlol/system-prompts-and-models-of-ai-tools/main/v0%20Prompts%20and%20Tools/Prompt.txt) (*unofficial copy*, May 2026) is the most numeric: 3–5 colors, two font families at most, body line height 1.4–1.6, nothing under 14px, and a required `GenerateDesignInspiration` call before design work. A search of it finds no hover, focus or animation rule. The [Lovable copy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/x1xhlol/system-prompts-and-models-of-ai-tools/main/Lovable/Agent%20Prompt.txt) (*unofficial copy*, September 2025) centers everything on tokens and shadcn variants. The [Bolt open-source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/x1xhlol/system-prompts-and-models-of-ai-tools/main/Open%20Source%20prompts/Bolt/Prompt.txt) and [Replit](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/x1xhlol/system-prompts-and-models-of-ai-tools/main/Replit/Prompt.txt) copies have almost no visual guidance, though the first is an open-source variant and the second predates Replit Design. No Figma Make copy was found, and Stitch appears only through a [secondary write-up of a leak](https://dev.to/yang_ella_f2a3e16ccb54550/google-stitch-system-prompt-leaked-analysis-and-insights-23dp).

Official documents are more dependable. [OpenAI's GPT-5.4 guide](https://developers.openai.com/blog/designing-delightful-frontends-with-gpt-5-4) is the fullest rule set: one H1, six sections at most, one primary call to action above the fold, one accent by default, no cards by default, and Playwright to check renders. [Anthropic's Opus 4.8 guide](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/prompting-claude-opus-4-8) says that model needs less design prompting than its predecessors. State handling lives mostly outside builder prompts, in [Vercel's guidelines](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines/main/README.md): visible focus, 24px hit targets (44px on mobile), designed empty and error states, reduced motion, and never `transition: all`. Popular rules collections such as [awesome-cursorrules](https://github.com/PatrickJS/awesome-cursorrules) mostly encode stack conventions, by a summarized read of its index, with the [Toss rule](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PatrickJS/awesome-cursorrules/main/rules/toss-style-design-system.mdc) a rare restrained product-UI exception. Across all these sources, the near-universal rules are tokens over ad hoc values, specific briefs over vague ones, following an existing brand over any default, responsive layout, no default purple, and few accents and typefaces. [Figma warns](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/33665861260823-Add-guidelines-to-Figma-Make) that more context can confuse the model.

### Redesign routes add what build skills lack

Five packs treat existing sites differently from new builds. [Hallmark's redesign reference](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Nutlope/hallmark/main/skills/hallmark/references/redesign.md) is the most complete. It preserves content, wiring (links, forms, handlers, ids, test hooks, analytics), brand and words, and it sets three non-blending depths: full, restyle and polish. **`sloplint --before` records every link, form, field, handler, script, queried selector, heading and figure, and `sloplint --kept` fails the run if a recorded item disappears without being logged.** Its finish review also asks what the old page did better, and the route runs only when the user names Hallmark, since edits to an existing design otherwise stay in the project's own system. [Taste-skill](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/main/skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md) inspects into a before folder, works in order of effect against risk (type, color, frame, states, stock components), and defines proof as re-inspecting, comparing at every width and clicking through previously working links and forms. [Impeccable](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pbakaus/impeccable/main/skill/SKILL.src.md) splits refinement from redesign and forbids blending them. [avoid-ai-design](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/funboy322/avoid-ai-design/main/SKILL.md) separates surgical passes from rebuilds and accepts a clean audit as a valid result. [ibelick's improve-ui](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ibelick/ui-skills/main/skills/improve-ui/SKILL.md) never edits product source and prefers no finding to an unsupported one. By contrast, [frontend-design](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anthropics/skills/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md) now mentions existing UI but has no baseline, preservation or regression step, and [ui-ux-pro-max's SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill/main/.claude/skills/ui-ux-pro-max/SKILL.md) has no redesign workflow.

**Inference:** The shared pattern is baseline, invariants, depth, change, diff, and it addresses a risk visual rules miss: silently breaking working behavior. All these routes assume source access. Hallmark can only audit a live URL with no source behind it, and [Impeccable's URL scan and Chrome overlay](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pbakaus/impeccable/main/README.md) report findings rather than rewrite a site.

## Ban lists name a real cluster, then push models to the next default

The first-wave tells have broad agreement: Inter or default sans, purple or indigo gradients, identical rounded or icon-topped card grids, a centered hero with a pill badge, colored left or top card borders, glassmorphism, grey low-contrast dark mode, all-caps eyebrow labels, and filler copy with invented statistics ([Krebs](https://www.adriankrebs.ch/blog/design-slop/); [Capital & Compute](https://capitalandcompute.net/blog/fix-ai-slop-design/); [Anthropic, *vendor*](https://claude.com/blog/improving-frontend-design-through-skills)). The best count comes from Adrian Krebs, whose deterministic checks on 1,590 Show HN pages put **22% at four or more patterns, 32% at two or three and 46% at one or none**. He estimates false positives at 5–10% by a manual pass, treats a high score as a template look rather than proof of AI, and considers the result uninspired rather than harmful. His post is inconsistent on sample size, and it has no ground truth for authorship.

The second wave arrived once the first was banned: cream with serif type and a terracotta accent near #D97757, near-black with one neon accent, and broadsheet hairlines with zero radius ([Capital & Compute](https://capitalandcompute.net/blog/fix-ai-slop-design/)). Anthropic now states the mechanism directly. Its [Opus 4.8 guide](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/prompting-claude-opus-4-8) describes a house style of cream near `#F4F1EA`, serif display type, italic accents and terracotta or amber that suits editorial briefs and fails dashboards, dev tools, fintech, healthcare and enterprise apps, and it says generic bans just move the model to another fixed palette. Its [skill](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anthropics/skills/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md) names the terracotta as Anthropic's own interface accent. Fraunces shows the cycle: the [2025 cookbook](https://platform.claude.com/cookbook/coding-prompting-for-frontend-aesthetics) offers it as an option, the Opus 4.8 guide lists it in the default style, and the [October 2026 Claude Design copy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/asgeirtj/system_prompts_leaks/main/Anthropic/claude-design/claude-design.md) (*unofficial copy*) calls it overused. HN users report the new look as recognizable ([smusamashah](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48507500); [efficax](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48497957); [bensyverson](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49642823)).

Enforcement varies. [Anthropic's skill](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anthropics/skills/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md) lets the brief's own words override every warning. [Hallmark](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Nutlope/hallmark/main/skills/hallmark/SKILL.md) has a 16-item non-waivable floor covering tokens, no `transition: all`, focus-visible, reduced-motion fallbacks, no invented metrics or logos, and no eyebrows. [avoid-ai-design](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/funboy322/avoid-ai-design/main/SKILL.md) tiers tells by who notices them and adds rendered silhouette and squint tests. [Taste-skill](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/main/skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md) bans eyebrows, monospace outside code, index numbering, thin rules, gradients, resting shadows and glass, and admits that some bans are its owner's taste. [Its study](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/main/skills/taste-skill/references/research.md) of 247 gallery sites, scored by model reviewers from still frames, supports some bans (icon feature cards 1% among sites scoring 7.5 or higher against 28% among those under 6.5; tiny tracked caps 16% against 57%) and contradicts others, since monospace labels, index numbers and hairlines were more common on award sites. The weak group held only 23 measurable sites, and the source says the sample reflects fashion as much as quality.

The lists also contradict each other, as this sample shows.

Whether the tells mean AI at all is disputed. HN users argue gradients and centered heroes predate LLMs and come from TailwindUI and shadcn ([nomdep](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47865416)), and that non-AI shadcn generator output reads the same ([waterTanuki](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47871728)). A replier notes that framework defaults do not explain the contrast problem ([toraway](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47866612)).

Professional critics target something deeper than tells. [NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-prototyping/) found outputs polished at a glance and weak on inspection, with poor grouping and hierarchy, overused color, poor contrast and inconsistent margins. [Design Theater](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.22928) found that about a quarter of stated design rationale never reached the interface. [EvoGenUI-Bench](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.29387) places multi-turn presentation failures in information architecture. Reports of absent empty, loading and error states come from unmeasured, partly vendor [search summaries](https://www.13labs.au/guides/beyond-the-prototype-ui). Fabricated statistics need separate removal, since visual rules cannot catch them ([Capital & Compute](https://capitalandcompute.net/blog/fix-ai-slop-design/)), and Hallmark and taste-skill make invented proof a hard ban.

**Inference:** Tells work as a density signal with a shelf life of months. Krebs's April 2026 checks omit the cream-and-terracotta look that a September 2026 guide treats as a main tell. Many rule conflicts reflect surface type or era more than opinion, as [OpenAI's](https://developers.openai.com/blog/designing-delightful-frontends-with-gpt-5-4) split between brand pages and product UI suggests. Removing visual tells can also raise a page's apparent trustworthiness without fixing hierarchy, states or flows, though nobody has tested this.

## Seven direction methods exist, and model self-selection is the documented failure

|---|---|---|---|

| Commit to a distinctive look | [frontend-design](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anthropics/skills/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md) | Subject's industry, materials and vernacular, then model judgment | Illustrative side-by-sides |
| Intake questions | [Impeccable init](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pbakaus/impeccable/main/README.md), [gstack consultation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/garrytan/gstack/main/design-consultation/SKILL.md.tmpl) | Answers saved as context for later steps | None found |

| Scripted or random draw from a catalog | [Impeccable v4](https://impeccable.style/research), [taste-skill](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/main/skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md), [Hallmark](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Nutlope/hallmark/main/skills/hallmark/SKILL.md) | Model or brief proposes, script assigns | Impeccable's own experiments, one rater |

The closest thing to an experiment is [Impeccable's July 2026 campaign](https://impeccable.style/research) (*vendor*): about 30 skill iterations, about 200 sampled concepts and about $2,600 of evaluations. **In it, 30 of 35 responses across sixteen creative framings proposed the same concept, rejecting first ideas landed the model on its second choice, and the first-listed option won 27 of 30 packets when the model, a simulated user or a rejection rule chose.** The result was a script that picks among relevant ideas the model has already proposed. Curating the catalog took real work: 172 of 375 entries were rejected as of July 21. Its [home page](https://impeccable.style/) describes weighing directions against product ideas, which reads differently from random assignment, and the notes did not resolve this from the code.

First-party guidance points the same way from a different angle. [Anthropic's Opus 4.8](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/prompting-claude-opus-4-8) and [Sonnet 5](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/prompting-claude-sonnet-5) guides name two reliable fixes: a concrete specification, or proposing four distinct directions (background hex, accent hex, typeface, one-line rationale) and building only the chosen one. [gstack's design-shotgun](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/garrytan/gstack/main/design-shotgun/SKILL.md.tmpl) defaults to three variants, confirms text concepts before spending image calls, and reworks one of two variants whose headlines could be swapped unnoticed. Private briefs written by the model, as in the [v0](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/x1xhlol/system-prompts-and-models-of-ai-tools/main/v0%20Prompts%20and%20Tools/Prompt.txt) and [Lovable](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/x1xhlol/system-prompts-and-models-of-ai-tools/main/Lovable/Agent%20Prompt.txt) copies, form a separate camp. [NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/vague-prototyping/) rates naming a recognized style above describing its traits, both above words like trendy, and advises against asking AI to copy a specific brand.

**Inference:** A private brief is unlikely to break defaults, because the same model writes it, and generating options adds cost without variety if the agent then picks its own favorite. This rests on one vendor's data plus Anthropic's guidance, which cites no test. Direction steps also create friction, and the Opus 4.8 guide elsewhere advises fewer human interactions in coding products, so these two pieces of advice need balancing.

### Style-to-context tables are unvalidated, and the audience matters as much as the industry

The main published mapping is [ui-ux-pro-max's reasoning table](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill/main/src/ui-ux-pro-max/data/ui-reasoning.csv): Banking gets Minimalism and Swiss plus Accessible and Ethical, general SaaS gets Glassmorphism plus Flat, Educational App gets Claymorphism, Creative Agency gets Brutalism. **No study validates any such table against conversion, trust or usability.** Researcher checks of its 192 rows found internal contradictions. Healthcare App recommends Neumorphism, which [the same skill's style table](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill/main/src/ui-ux-pro-max/data/styles.csv) rates high accessibility risk and excludes from critical accessibility, while the row also requires WCAG AAA. Minimalism and Swiss appears in 35% of rows, and Reasoning and Confidence fields are blank in 161 rows. One [critic](https://lilting.ch/en/articles/ui-ux-pro-max-skill-comparison) warns of industry-looking mass production, without having run the skill.

The better-sourced guidance gives cautions rather than assignments. NN/g says [neobrutalism](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/neobrutalism/) suits distinct personalities but can overwhelm users and hinder accessibility, says to keep [brutalism](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/brutalism-antidesign/) out of hierarchy, navigation and interaction, and advises avoiding antidesign for most products. It wants [glassmorphism](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/glassmorphism/) contrast tested against real backgrounds. It has no bento article that a site search could find. Its [tone study](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/tone-voice-users/) undercuts blanket seriousness rules: among 100 US adults reading text-only copy, playful insurance copy lost trust, while casual banking and humorous home-security copy gained it. [Reinecke and Gajos](https://eecs.harvard.edu/~kgajos/papers/2014/reinecke14visual.shtml) collected 2.4 million ratings from nearly 40,000 people and found color and complexity preferences varying by country and education, which an industry row cannot capture. Impeccable's [surface classes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pbakaus/impeccable/main/skill/SKILL.src.md) and [interface-design's](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dammyjay93/interface-design/main/.claude/skills/interface-design/SKILL.md) focus on product UI offer a different axis, based on the page's job rather than the company's sector.

**Inference:** Exclusions travel better than prescriptions. Avoiding low contrast on critical tasks has NN/g support, while a claim that SaaS means glassmorphism has none. A flat ban on playful design in serious industries goes beyond NN/g's data, which is mixed and limited to copy.

## References trade range for anchoring, and the best galleries gate agents

Workflows use five channels. Paid MCP servers come from [Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/mcp) (OAuth, 60 requests per 60 seconds per user) and [Refero](https://doc.refero.design/mcp/getting-started.md) (up to 8,000 calls a month). Unofficial wrappers include [awwwards-mcp](https://github.com/INSANE0777/Awwwards-mcp/wiki), a scraper limited to robots-allowed paths at one request per second, and [referro-mcp](https://github.com/CalmOli/referro-mcp), which calls reverse-engineered undocumented endpoints. Approved browsing appears in [gstack](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/garrytan/gstack/main/design-consultation/SKILL.md.tmpl), where the user approves each site and sign-in walls or bot checks get skipped. User-supplied screenshots appear in [Claude Design](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/14604416-get-started-with-claude-design). Token extraction appears in [Dembrandt](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dembrandt/dembrandt/main/README.md), [awesome-design-md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md/main/README.md) and [Firecrawl's branding format](https://docs.firecrawl.dev/features/scrape). No independent audit of extraction fidelity was found.

Access varies sharply. The fetch column reflects one fetch-tool request and one plain curl per page on 2026-11-02, with no retries and no altered user agent, so a real browser could behave differently.

**Repo check:** `extension/src/chat/lib/prompt.ts` line 18 uses design inspiration from Awwwards, Behance and Dribbble as its example of multi-tab work, and `browserSchemas.ts` line 86 uses Dribbble shots as a tab-purpose example. The words `inspiration` and `references` switch on the `browser` tool group (`toolRuntime.ts` line 64). **Inference:** TamperSloth is not a curl client, because it reads tabs in the user's own browser, so the observed blocks may not occur. That changes the technical picture without changing the terms, and whether a user-directed agent counts as an automated means under Dribbble's or Mobbin's wording is a legal question these notes did not research. Refero's bar on tokens in client-side or distributed software appears to rule out a shared key inside a shipped extension without a separate agreement, and even a user's own token stored in an extension could count as client-side. The Mobbin §3(4) wording sits awkwardly beside its own MCP product and warrants legal review before anything ships.

Copying is treated as a risk, unevenly. Figma [disabled Make Design in July 2024](https://9to5mac.com/2024/07/19/figma-explains-why-ai-kept-making-copies-of-apples-weather-app/) after near-copies of Apple's Weather app, later blaming unvetted design-system assets. [Refero's reference lock](https://github.com/referodesign/refero_skill/blob/master/skills/refero-design/SKILL.md) names one primary source, 3–5 traits to preserve, 1–2 borrowed details and a reject list, and warns against averaging references. Its style library excludes dashboards, auth, settings and iOS screens, and it tells agents not to run generic design skills as a parallel authority. [Mobbin's guide](https://mobbin.com/mcp.md) tells agents to compare several products, keep names and URLs, separate what is visible from interpretation, and treat a recurring pattern as a convention rather than a proven practice. [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/research) kept an instruction to borrow a form's skeleton rather than its surface. Brand-file collections and cloning tools run the other way, making imitation the default, and [Dembrandt](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dembrandt/dembrandt/main/README.md) warns against reproducing brand identities while shipping a `--stealth` flag.

Evidence that references beat prompt-only work is thin. **No controlled study compares them for coding agents with independent human judges.** In favor: [Vologdin and Bakaev](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-96177-9_18) (*search summary*) report multimodal prompts raising mean evaluations by 9.1% to 50.9% on image models, with simple wireframes beating detailed screenshots. [NN/g's prompt ladder](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-prototyping/) found detailed prompts approaching the human design and a text-plus-Figma prompt most accurate, without numeric scores. [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/research) saw reference cards in its strongest builds but did not isolate their effect. Against: [Wadinambiarachchi et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.11164v1) (CHI 2024, N=60) found that image search and, more strongly, generative AI raised design fixation in human ideation. [Chen et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.05870v1) saw novices make superficial modifications rather than new structures.

**Inference:** The claim that references improve output is plausible and unproven for agents, while the claim that references anchor has better experimental support, though with humans. Raw screenshots look like the weakest form, on limited evidence. The human advantage in [Design Creativity Bench](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.11539v1) may partly reflect access to reference libraries that the models lacked, though the paper does not separate this.

Self-critique deserves special caution. Anthropic's Labs team reports that [agents skew positive grading their own work](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/harness-design-long-running-apps), and PerceptUI shows consistency far below average accuracy when input order flips. **Inference:** The common factor among positive reports is an external source of decisions, which is a pattern across anecdotes rather than a tested conclusion. Causes are probably additive: frameworks homogenized the web ([Goree et al.](https://vision.soic.indiana.edu/papers/websimilarity2021chi.pdf), excerpt only), training data absorbed that, tools hard-code the same stack, and vague prompts leave defaults in place. Because every model cleared 90% appropriateness in Design Creativity Bench, better requirements alone may not produce distinctiveness. Disputes over whether skills help may partly reflect audience, with non-designers reporting gains and experienced reviewers seeing a new default, though no study segments by expertise.

## A live page reverses the repo assumptions behind most of this advice

Three product groups touch this space, and only one shares TamperSloth's constraints. In-place overlays include [ClickRemix](https://clickremix.com/) (*vendor*, beta, discloses no failure modes), [Stylebot](https://stylebot.dev/), whose CLI lets a coding agent inspect, apply CSS and screenshot with restorable version history, and [AI Style Upgrade](https://dev.to/eldad_zamler/restyling-a-legacy-website-without-touching-its-html-or-breaking-its-js-1gjg) (*vendor*, no test results), which leaves markup unchanged and skips properties controlled by the site's own JS or inline styles. Capture tools such as [Shuffle](https://shuffle.dev/blog/2026/04/ai-website-redesign-browser-extension/) generate a new codebase and so avoid every constraint below. Source-mapped editors such as [Cursor's](https://cursor.com/blog/browser-visual-editor) depend on code a third-party page does not expose. **No independent benchmark, success rate or failure study exists for any of them.** [Dark Reader](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/darkreader/darkreader/main/CONTRIBUTING.md), not AI but the most mature restyler examined, still needs per-site fixes.

The page keeps running, and that causes most of the difficulty. Unlayered normal styles beat layered ones regardless of specificity, and [the order reverses for `!important`](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/@layer). [Important inline styles beat every other author declaration, and transitions outrank everything](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Guides/Cascade/Introduction). [React warns](https://react.dev/learn/manipulating-the-dom-with-refs) that changing DOM nodes it manages can cause inconsistent visuals or crashes, while parts it has no reason to update are safe. [Generated class names](https://nextjs.org/docs/app/getting-started/css) make class selectors fragile, and [Playwright](https://playwright.dev/docs/best-practices) prefers roles and user-facing attributes for that reason. Dark Reader's [node-position watcher](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/darkreader/darkreader/main/src/inject/utils/dom.ts) reinserts removed nodes, discards its own mutations with `takeRecords()`, and pauses for 2 seconds after 10 attempts in 10 seconds so it does not fight the page forever. It also [walks shadow roots and handles `adoptedStyleSheets`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/darkreader/darkreader/main/src/inject/dynamic-theme/index.ts), because page CSS and `querySelectorAll` [do not reach shadow content](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Web_components/Using_shadow_DOM).

Content security policy splits by world. The [USER_SCRIPT world is exempt from page CSP](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/extensions/reference/api/userScripts), and [main-world scripts are not](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/extensions/develop/concepts/content-scripts). **Researcher test** (Chromium 152, main world, example.com, strict CSP via meta tag): `<style>` elements, `setAttribute('style', ...)`, `FontFace` loads and images were blocked, while `element.style`, `style.cssText` and `adoptedStyleSheets` were not. Whether the USER_SCRIPT exemption covers fonts and images referenced from injected CSS was not tested, and that decides whether webfonts are usable at all.

Overlays run into the top layer. [`showModal()` makes the rest of the document inert](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/HTMLDialogElement/showModal). **Researcher test:** an overlay at `z-index: 2147483647` lost the hit test to a modal, even outside the dialog's box; an injected button could not take focus; and a manual popover shown after the modal was also not hit-testable. [WXT](https://wxt.dev/guide/key-concepts/content-script-ui.html) frames injection as Integrated, Shadow Root or IFrame, and notes that `all: initial` does not reset the root font size, so rem-based UI changes scale across sites. A researcher test confirmed `1rem` measured 10px inside a shadow root when the root font was 10px, with inherited color and page custom properties crossing the boundary. [web.dev](https://web.dev/articles/shadowdom-v1) documents the same inheritance, along with retargeted focus and events. Some further pitfalls, such as an ancestor's `overflow: hidden` clipping a popover, come from [developer postmortems seen only as search summaries](https://dev.to/michael-yousrie/things-shadow-dom-does-not-isolate-3jj3).

The agent has to read the site's design first, and computed styles are the reliable base. [Dembrandt](https://github.com/dembrandt/dembrandt) reads computed styles after rendering, states limits (some dynamic content missed, hover and focus sampled, dark mode by flag, no canvas or WebGL), and exports W3C tokens. [Chrome's CSS Overview](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/devtools/css-overview) groups colors, low-contrast text, fonts and media queries. `cssRules` [throws on cross-origin sheets](https://www.w3.org/TR/cssom-1/), so media queries and hover rules are the hard part. A researcher test in Chromium 152 found custom properties enumerable through `getComputedStyle`. No source measures extraction accuracy.

Verification shifts from pass/fail to before-and-after. [axe-core](https://github.com/dequelabs/axe-core) finds on average 57% of WCAG issues by its own figure, and it reports uncertain cases as incomplete. [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/quickref/?versions=2.2&levels=aa) gives the thresholds a restyle can break: 4.5:1 and 3:1 contrast, 3:1 non-text contrast, reflow at 320 CSS px, text spacing, visible and unobscured focus, and 24px targets. [Playwright's visual comparison](https://playwright.dev/docs/test-snapshots) depends on a same-environment baseline. [`captureVisibleTab`](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/extensions/reference/api/tabs) allows 2 calls per second and captures only the visible area. The one published checklist from a restyler is [vendor-authored](https://dev.to/eldad_zamler/restyling-a-legacy-website-without-touching-its-html-or-breaking-its-js-1gjg) and warns that overriding `display` can break script-driven behavior.

**Repo check:** The outer loop already exists: inspect, author, execute, verify, confirm scope, save, reload and verify (`skills.ts` lines 24–32). The instruments are thinner, which qualifies the research note's claim that the gap is design knowledge rather than plumbing. `getElementDetails` returns seven computed properties (color, size, family, background, radius, padding, shadow) for up to 10 elements, with no weight, line height, gap or custom properties (`tools.ts` lines 618–660). `takeScreenshot` captures the visible viewport only. A search of `extension/src` finds no `Emulation.` CDP call, no `insertCSS` call and no axe-core, so multi-width, dark-mode and audit checks have no dedicated tool today, although the manifest grants `scripting`, `userScripts`, `debugger` and `<all_urls>`, and `evaluateJavaScript` can run custom extraction. `skills.ts` line 25 says to inspect styles only if adding UI, and line 30 recommends `document_idle` for UI additions. Running the `initialToolGroups` regexes on `redesign this page`, `make this site look better`, `give this a new theme` and `add a filter chip to this table` each yields `browsing` and `input` only, while `restyle this page` adds `scripting` and `visual`. The `input` group does include `takeMarkedScreenshot`. A script must be one self-contained named function in a sandbox without page globals (`skills.ts` line 36). `prompt.ts` line 32 bans custom overlays for walkthrough guidance only. Page text is treated as untrusted (`prompt.ts` line 40). Playbooks [ship inside the extension](../extension/playbooks/README.md), so a change needs a new build and store release, the whole file goes to the model on load, a `tools` field can add tool groups, and `lastVerified` is required.

**Inference:** The table below maps common repo-era advice to a live page. Its left column draws on the skills above plus the researcher's general knowledge, not a survey.

| Common advice | Status | What would have to change |
|---|---|---|
| Choose a framework, component library or Tailwind | Gone | Output is CSS plus minimal vanilla DOM; utilities exist only if the site ships them |
| Install motion, icon or component packages | Gone | Everything is hand-written inside one self-contained function |
| Pick palette and typefaces from the subject | Reversed | Site tokens come first; new values only when a new look is wanted, remapping roles rather than per-element values |
| Avoid `!important` | Reversed | It is normal here, and [Dark Reader requires it](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/darkreader/darkreader/main/CONTRIBUTING.md), with layer order flipping under it |
| Edit components, use semantic HTML | Changed | Override styles on markup the agent does not own; attribute patches can be overwritten on re-render |
| Define breakpoints, mobile first | Changed | Breakpoints already exist and have to be discovered |
| Lead with a hero, reorder sections, budget words | Limited | Landing recipes do not fit an inbox or dashboard; CSS reordering risks focus order |
| Write specific copy | Limited | Site text is dynamic, possibly localized and not the agent's; injected labels are its only copy |
| Webfonts and generated images | Mostly gone | Depends on the untested CSP question; site fonts, CSS and inline SVG need no fetch |
| Dev server, git, CI | Replaced | Execute-screenshot-adjust in the tab, idempotent runs, a saved script with URL scope and an off switch |
| Baseline against source | Changed | Hallmark's functional inventory has to run on the rendered DOM, including controls and text |
| Pixel diff against a baseline | Inverted | Useful only on regions meant to stay unchanged, since a redesign changes everything else |
| Done when shipped | Changed | The site ships changes underneath, so a saved redesign has to leave the page usable when selectors stop matching |

**Inference:** Hierarchy, restraint, visible focus, reduced motion, contrast and rendered critique survive with little change. Live pages also add concerns no code-project skill mentions: staying within the user's scope and URL pattern, not breaking what the site's own scripts rely on, keeping changes reversible and tagged, checking which states exist before styling them, and telling the user the change is local to their browser. CSS rules survive re-renders where DOM edits need observers, which argues for treating structural change as the riskier tier. An unlayered sheet should beat a page's layered normal rules, and `insertCSS` with `origin: "USER"` plus `!important` should beat all author rules, but neither was tested. A restyle saved at `document_idle` would show the old design first. Candidate mounting rules (no component for restyling, a shadow root that lets inheritance through for native-looking additions, a reset shadow root for tool UI, an iframe for heavy UI) are one researcher's synthesis, untested across sites. They are inputs to a decision rather than a decision.

## Conclusion: what a future playbook inherits and what it must settle

Five findings have the firmest support: model output converges, bans relocate the default, models judge their own design poorly, stated rationales overclaim what was built, and passing a tell-checker is a different thing from being judged better. Only the first and fourth rest on academic measurement. The second and fifth come from vendors conceding against interest, and the third from a vendor report plus an A/B-prediction benchmark. None of them concerns live pages, and most of the measured work studies new pages built from scratch, so the evidence cannot say what good looks like for TamperSloth's case.

**Inference:** Two implications follow. First, the direction methods above nearly all assume that the agent starts from a blank identity, while a live page supplies one that only the inherit method respects, and on a third-party page the owner's brief is missing too. Second, the most portable material from the repos is procedural rather than aesthetic, because a preservation inventory holds its value while a ban list decays, and that decay clashes with a playbook format needing a store release for each change.

The questions below stay open by design, because the user's process, which arrives later, can answer many of them. They are neutral on what the answer should be.

| Open question | Why the evidence does not settle it |
|---|---|
| Which jobs are in scope: a personal restyle of someone else's site, a native-looking added component, an owner's redesign proposal? | Skills assume an owned project. Preservation bar, copy rights and whether a direction step is needed all differ by job, and no source covers the first two |
| How far may a change move from the site's identity, and who sets that depth? | Hallmark's and Impeccable's non-blending depths exist for source projects only, with no live-page evidence for any default |
