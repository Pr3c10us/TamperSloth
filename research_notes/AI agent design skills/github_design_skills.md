# Public GitHub repositories containing AI agent design skills (UI/UX and frontend visual design)

Method note: star counts, push dates and licenses come from the GitHub API on 2026-11-02. "Tree size" is the sum of blob sizes from the GitHub recursive tree API on the same date. Skill text was read from raw files as text. Nothing was installed or executed. Text is paraphrased, with one direct quote, because of a copyright limit stricter than the brief. Stars and installs are adoption signals only, not evidence of output quality.

## Which design-focused skills and skill packs are most popular?

### Takeaway
By stars, the design-specific leaders are ui-ux-pro-max (144.1K), taste-skill (108.3K), and impeccable (100.0K), with Anthropic's frontend-design sitting inside a 186.6K general repo, and it is the most-installed long-standing design skill on skills.sh (1.1M). Two non-skill design repos also rank high: awesome-design-md (125.2K) and open-design (105.5K). Install counts and stars disagree badly, so neither ranks quality.

### Inferences
- Skills.sh installs and GitHub stars measure different things. A 25-star repo outranking frontend-design suggests automated or partner-driven installs, but no evidence confirms the cause.
- The top of the category is very young: most leading repos were created in 2026, and all of the top seven were pushed within the last week. Active maintenance is the norm, so staleness is mainly a problem in the awesome lists and the smaller repos.

### Gaps
- Star-history data was not pulled, so growth rates and any star inflation cannot be assessed.
- I could not confirm a GitHub repo for uizze.sh's `ui-taste`, so it stays outside the ranked table.
- Skills.sh does not show weekly installs on the two detail pages fetched.

## What does each skill's instruction text actually say?

### Takeaway
The top skills share a process of grounding in the subject, committing to a plan, building, then checking rendered output, and they share an accessibility floor. They disagree on specifics such as how much motion to use, corner radius, and glass. The biggest split is philosophy: ui-ux-pro-max retrieves category-matched recommendations, while Anthropic, Impeccable, taste-skill and Hallmark treat category defaults as the problem.

### Cited Findings

**Anthropic frontend-design** (1,516 words, single file, last changed 2026-09-03)

- Process: identify subject, audience and job; write a plan with 4–6 named hex colors, typefaces and roles, a layout concept with ASCII wireframes, and principles; review the plan against the brief and revise anything generic; build; critique with screenshots; remove one element — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anthropics/skills/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md).
- Numbers: line length under 80 characters, one or two families, and one orchestrated motion moment. For the type scale it defers to The Elements of Typographic Style, and it gives no spacing or duration values — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anthropics/skills/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md).
- It names five clusters of generated-looking design, including cream near #F4F1EA with terracotta near #D97757, which it identifies as Anthropic's own interface accent. The brief's own words always override these warnings — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anthropics/skills/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md).
- Core restraint rule: "Spend your boldness in one place." — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anthropics/skills/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md).

**ui-ux-pro-max** (SKILL.md 2,118 words; quick reference 3,833 words)

- Process: extract product type, audience, style keywords and detected stack; run `search.py --design-system` for new work; optionally persist a `MASTER.md` with page overrides; run supplementary domain and stack searches; retry an empty search once and never present it as data — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill/main/.claude/skills/ui-ux-pro-max/SKILL.md).
- Ten prioritized rule categories run from accessibility down to charts, with contrast 4.5:1, targets 44×44px with 8px+ spacing, CLS under 0.1, body 16px, line-height 1.5, and bottom nav of five or fewer — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill/main/.claude/skills/ui-ux-pro-max/SKILL.md).
- More numbers: focus rings 2–4px, tap feedback within 100ms, per-frame work about 16ms, 4pt/8dp spacing, exits at about 60–70% of enter duration, stagger 30–50ms per item, chart contrast at least 3:1 — [quick reference](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill/main/src/ui-ux-pro-max/templates/base/quick-reference.md).
- Dials for variance, motion and density each run 1–10. Density picks a spacing scale of 24–96, 16–64 or 8–32px — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill/main/.claude/skills/ui-ux-pro-max/SKILL.md).
- Row 1 of its reasoning data recommends Hero + Features + CTA, glassmorphism plus flat design, and trust blue for general SaaS — [ui-reasoning.csv](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill/main/src/ui-ux-pro-max/data/ui-reasoning.csv).

**Impeccable** (SKILL source 1,646 words; craft floor 1,009; new-work 10,009; critique 7,409; audit 1,261)

- Process: run the `context` launcher once to load PRODUCT.md, DESIGN.md and surface brief; load the command's playbook; read the craft floor immediately before any UI edit; check the rendered result, batching fixes. Surfaces are classed by what visitor success looks like: Persuade, Operate, Read or Experience — [SKILL source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pbakaus/impeccable/main/skill/SKILL.src.md).
- Numbers: body and placeholder contrast at least 4.5:1, large text at least 3:1, measure 65–75ch, display max 6rem, tracking floor -0.04em. Eyebrows are treated as an absolute ban, while most other items can be earned back by the brief — [craft floor](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pbakaus/impeccable/main/skill/reference/craft-floor.md).
- Its typography playbook uses a wider 45–75ch range and a 16px body floor — [typeset](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pbakaus/impeccable/main/skill/reference/typeset.md).
- Motion bands: 100–150ms feedback, 150–300ms routine state, 300–500ms layout or overlay, 500–800ms a focal entrance — [animate](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pbakaus/impeccable/main/skill/reference/animate.md).
- Audit scores 5 dimensions 0–4 for a total out of 20, with five rating bands and P0–P3 severity. It reports only and does not fix — [audit](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pbakaus/impeccable/main/skill/reference/audit.md).
- Critique scores Nielsen's 10 heuristics 0–4, adjusting the maximum when heuristics are marked not applicable — [critique](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pbakaus/impeccable/main/skill/reference/critique.md).

**taste-skill v2** (SKILL.md 6,670 words; about 288 KB of references)

- Ten-step run: read the brief; inventory 8–12 things only this client has; write the page as claims with proof; deal 3 of 20 directions by script; write an idea sentence that fails if a competitor fits it; write the copy deck; build without motion; gate with lint and inspector; critique screenshots for up to three rounds; add motion; report — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/main/skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md).
- Type: headlines at most two lines at every width; about 32 characters is safe; display tracking -0.02 to -0.05em, leading 0.85–1.05; text 16–20px, leading 1.45–1.6, 45–75 characters per line; five sizes at most, two families at most — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/main/skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md).
- Space and media: margins 16–32px, section gaps 120–240px on desktop, at least four real pictures, at most one near-empty screen — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/main/skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md).
- Motion is mandatory: fewer than eight moving elements fails and under sixteen warns; section parts stagger 60–90ms and rise 16–32px — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/main/skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md).
- Colour: ground 70–90% of the page, accent under 5% of any screen — [colour](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/main/skills/taste-skill/references/colour.md).
- Critique uses Awwwards-style weights of 40/30/20/10 and fourteen passes — [critique](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/main/skills/taste-skill/references/critique.md).
- The rule set bans eyebrows, monospace outside code, index numbering, thin rules, gradients, resting shadows, glass, tracked caps and styled accent words. It openly says some bans are the owner's taste and go against award-site fashion — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/main/skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md).
- The older dials (variance, motion, density) now live in [taste-skill-v1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill-classics/main/skills/taste-skill-v1/SKILL.md) in a [31-star classics repo](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill-classics) and do not appear in the v2 SKILL.md, so [roundups describing dials](https://www.paralect.com/skills/top-design-skills) are out of date.

**Hallmark v2.0.0** (SKILL.md 2,889 words; craft 3,301 words)

- Process: pre-flight the project; ask once for material, audience, use and tone; inventory real, pictured and absent material; deal three themes; state a Picks block; build the hero alone and review it in fresh context; lint until clean; run a fresh-context finish review — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Nutlope/hallmark/main/skills/hallmark/SKILL.md).
- A 16-item non-waivable Floor covers tokens for all colours and fonts, no `transition: all`, focus-visible, reduced-motion fallbacks, no invented metrics or logos, no eyebrows, hero visible at 1280×800, and about 80 words per screen — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Nutlope/hallmark/main/skills/hallmark/SKILL.md).
- Page budget: hero headline at most 7 words or 50 characters, one sentence of at most 12 words, 6–9 sections, at most 450 visible words — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Nutlope/hallmark/main/skills/hallmark/SKILL.md).
- Craft numbers: 1.25 scale from 16px; display 84px at 1440; display tracking -0.02 to -0.04em; line-height 1.0–1.15, body 1.5–1.65, measure 45–75ch; accent under 5%; contrast 4.5:1 with a 7:1 target or APCA Lc 60; spacing 2/4/8/12/16/24/40/64/96/144; durations 120/220/420ms with exits at 60–75%; one reveal of 0.4–0.8s — [craft](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Nutlope/hallmark/main/skills/hallmark/references/craft.md).

**Emil Kowalski, emil-design-eng** (3,921 words; prose only)

- Reviews must be a Before/After/Why table. Animation decisions follow a sequence: should it animate, what purpose, what easing, how fast — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/emilkowalski/skills/main/skills/emil-design-eng/SKILL.md).
- Frequency rule: actions used 100+ times a day get no animation, and keyboard-initiated actions never animate — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/emilkowalski/skills/main/skills/emil-design-eng/SKILL.md).
- Durations: press 100–160ms, tooltips 125–200ms, dropdowns 150–250ms, modals and drawers 200–500ms, with general UI under 300ms. Press scale 0.95–0.98, entrances from scale 0.9 or higher, never ease-in on UI, blur under 20px, and named cubic-bezier curves such as `0.23, 1, 0.32, 1` — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/emilkowalski/skills/main/skills/emil-design-eng/SKILL.md).

**Vercel web-design-guidelines**

- The skill is a 176-word wrapper that fetches rules at run time from another repo and reports findings as `file:line` — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vercel-labs/agent-skills/main/skills/web-design-guidelines/SKILL.md).
- The fetched rules are about 1,049 words of implementation checks across accessibility, focus, forms, animation, typography, performance, navigation, touch, theming, i18n and hydration. Examples: never `transition: all`, virtualize lists above 50 items, autoplay over 5 seconds needs controls, Title Case for headings and buttons — [command.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines/main/command.md).

**Smaller skills read**

- ibelick baseline-ui (508 words) uses MUST/SHOULD/NEVER constraints: no animation unless requested, feedback at most 200ms, no custom easing unless requested, no gradients unless requested, one accent per view — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ibelick/ui-skills/main/skills/baseline-ui/SKILL.md).
- avoid-ai-design (2,234 words) has P0/P1/P2 tiers by who notices the tell, a rendered silhouette test, a 25% squint test, and four success tests. It warns that passing a scanner does not prove quality — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/funboy322/avoid-ai-design/main/SKILL.md).
- interface-design (4,513 words) targets product UI: type ratios near 1.2, 1.25 or 1.333 from 14–16px, a 4 or 8px base unit, and one focal point per view — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dammyjay93/interface-design/main/.claude/skills/interface-design/SKILL.md).
- design-motion-principles (957 words) routes between three named practitioners' perspectives by context — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kylezantos/design-motion-principles/main/skills/design-motion-principles/SKILL.md).
- Google taste-design (1,771 words) writes DESIGN.md through Stitch. It sets a 44px tap target and 2.5rem card radius, and states a body minimum as both 1rem and 14px — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google-labs-code/stitch-skills/main/plugins/stitch-utilities/skills/taste-design/SKILL.md).
- Huashu (Chinese; first 60 lines read) requires three initial directions for any new design, even with a named brand, warns against the same cream-serif-terracotta default, and excludes production web apps from scope — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/alchaincyf/huashu-design/master/SKILL.md).

### Inferences

**Where they agree**

- Contrast of 4.5:1 and 3:1, 44px targets, 16px body, reduced-motion support and animating only transform and opacity recur widely. These come largely from WCAG and platform guidelines, so agreement here is weak evidence of design insight.
- Measures of 45–75ch appear in taste-skill, Hallmark and Impeccable's typeset playbook; Anthropic allows up to 80 characters.
- Not inventing proof is now a hard rule in Hallmark and taste-skill, and also appears in Impeccable and Google's taste-design.
- Several skills add a mechanism to stop outputs converging: taste-skill's draw, Hallmark's deal and log, Impeccable's dice and concept seed, Huashu's three directions, and Anthropic's check for what a similar prompt would produce.

**Where they conflict**

| Topic | Position A | Position B |
|---|---|---|
| Amount of motion | One moment (Anthropic, Impeccable), one reveal at most (Hallmark), none unless requested (baseline-ui) | Taste-skill requires section entrances and at least 8 movers; Anthropic calls per-section fade-and-rise a generated look |
| Corner radius | Impeccable's Codex block: cards 12–16px | Taste-skill: square or fully round, calling 9–16px a weak-site default; Google taste-design: 2.5rem |
| Glass | Banned or restricted in taste-skill and Impeccable | ui-ux-pro-max's first SaaS row recommends glassmorphism |
| Hairlines | Taste-skill bans them | Hallmark's surface ladder allows a 1px rule |
| Heading case | Vercel: Title Case | Anthropic: sentence case |
| Measure | Impeccable craft floor: 65–75ch | Impeccable typeset: 45–75ch (an internal inconsistency) |

- Ban lists shift as models adapt. Avoid-ai-design and Anthropic both describe a second layer of tasteful defaults that replaced purple gradients.

### Gaps
- Not read in full: Emil's other 15 skills, MengTo's 179 skills, Owl-Listener's 111 skills, plugin87's skills, the remainder of Huashu, and Impeccable's other references.
- Claims about specific file contents in those packs were therefore limited to names and sizes.

**Operational cautions, stated by the projects themselves**

- Impeccable's hooks run outside model approval, so the first edit can download the engine. New-design runs fetch directions from impeccable.style even when telemetry is off; turning telemetry off stops only the record of what was shown and chosen — [README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pbakaus/impeccable/main/README.md).
- Vercel's skill fetches its rules from a remote URL each time it runs — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vercel-labs/agent-skills/main/skills/web-design-guidelines/SKILL.md).
- Taste-skill's [detector thresholds](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/main/skills/taste-skill/scripts/lib/rules.mjs) hard-code colour families such as cream, terracotta and purple by HSL range.

### Inferences
- The most tool-heavy packs turn taste rules into checks an agent cannot argue with. Their rules are still opinions, and passing them does not establish quality, as both [taste-skill](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/main/skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md) and [avoid-ai-design](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/funboy322/avoid-ai-design/main/SKILL.md) state.
- A remote fetch at run time (Vercel, Impeccable) means skill behaviour can change without a local update, which matters when vetting these for reuse.

### Gaps
- Impeccable's 62-rule claim comes from its README. My attempt to count rules in the Rust source did not match its format, so the number was not independently verified.
- Hallmark's gate count was not established.

## Are there skills specifically for redesigning an existing site, and what do they do differently?

### Takeaway
Yes. Hallmark's `redesign` reference is the most complete, followed by taste-skill's merged route, Impeccable's refinement and redesign split, avoid-ai-design's audit and rewrite modes, and ibelick's read-only improve-ui. They differ from build skills by recording a baseline first, naming what must survive, setting a depth, and verifying that nothing regressed.

### Cited Findings

**Hallmark `redesign` and `audit`** ([redesign.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Nutlope/hallmark/main/skills/hallmark/references/redesign.md), 2,184 words)

- It runs only when the user names Hallmark. Otherwise edits to an existing design are done in the project's own system.
- Preserved: content, wiring (links, forms, handlers, ids, test hooks, analytics), files, brand and words unless a copy gate names them. Terms and disclaimers are never reworded.
- Three non-blending depths: full (content and wiring stay), restyle (layout stays), and polish (system and layout stay; only gate-named tells change).
- `sloplint --before` records every link, form, field, handler, test hook, script, queried selector, heading and figure, plus screenshots. `audit` stops at this stage and changes no project files.
- `sloplint --kept` fails the run if a recorded functional item goes missing, unless the drop is recorded in the stamp.
- It requires a clean git tree, never edits tests to pass, and needs approval for any file deletion. It writes `.hallmark/compare.html` and a handoff with before and after counts.
- The finish review also answers what the old page did better. Existing claims are kept and listed for the owner to verify.

**taste-skill** ([SKILL.md, Special cases](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/main/skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md))

- The standalone [`redesign-existing-projects`](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/blob/f6831c5df8ad546c2567535442f1691a823868e3/skills/redesign-skill/SKILL.md) was retired into the main skill; see also the [README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/main/README.md).
- Steps: inspect and lint into `.taste-skill/before`; a review stops after naming the three highest-impact changes; no framework migration; a restyle keeps the layout, while a redesign deals a full direction inside existing components.
- Order of work runs by effect against risk: type, colour, frame, states, then stock components.
- Proof means re-inspecting, comparing at every width, clicking through previously working links and forms, and reporting measurements side by side.

**Impeccable** ([SKILL source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pbakaus/impeccable/main/skill/SKILL.src.md), [README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pbakaus/impeccable/main/README.md), [polish](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pbakaus/impeccable/main/skill/reference/polish.md))

- Refinement preserves identity, behaviour, copy and out-of-scope areas. Redesign keeps product truth but treats the old look as an anti-reference and replaces DESIGN.md. The skill forbids blending the two.
- The README calls existing interfaces its strongest case, and narrow targets are recommended over whole pages.
- `document` captures the current system; `critique` and `audit` assess; `bolder`, `quieter`, `distill`, `typeset`, `layout` and others steer; `live` swaps variants in a running dev server.
- The detector scans URLs, and the Chrome extension overlays findings on any live page.

**Others**

- avoid-ai-design has `detect` and `rewrite` modes. Components and work inside a design system get a surgical pass, standalone pages get a rebuild, and multi-page work locks a DESIGN.md to prevent drift. It treats a clean audit as a valid result — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/funboy322/avoid-ai-design/main/SKILL.md).
- ibelick improve-ui never modifies product source. It traces one surface through its real code path, prefers no finding to an unsupported one, and writes self-contained plans for another agent — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ibelick/ui-skills/main/skills/improve-ui/SKILL.md).
- ui-ux-pro-max has no redesign workflow in SKILL.md. Its README offers a retheme prompt and an audit, map, migrate, verify checklist, and says results do not rewrite an app automatically — [README](https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill/blob/main/README.md).
- Emil's reviews use the Before/After/Why table — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/emilkowalski/skills/main/skills/emil-design-eng/SKILL.md). Its repo also has [improve-animations, review-animations, remove-animations and break-ui](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills/tree/main/skills), which were not read.
- Anthropic's description now covers reshaping existing UI, but the file has no baseline, preservation or regression step — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anthropics/skills/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md).

### Inferences
- The shared redesign pattern is: capture a baseline, list invariants, choose a depth, change, then diff against the baseline.
- Hallmark's functional inventory is the most concrete regression guard found, because it checks wiring mechanically instead of trusting the agent. It covers the risk specific to redesign that visual rules miss, which is silently breaking working behaviour.
- These redesign routes assume access to the source. [Hallmark](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Nutlope/hallmark/main/skills/hallmark/references/redesign.md) can only audit a live URL with no source behind it, and [Impeccable's](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pbakaus/impeccable/main/README.md) URL scan and Chrome overlay report findings rather than rewrite a site. A tool restyling third-party pages it does not own would have to adapt this model rather than copy it.

### Gaps
- The pre-retirement text of taste-skill's redesign skill was not recovered. Only its stub was read, and a third-party description of its earlier workflow is unverified.
- `extract-design-md`, `code-to-design` and `Manavarya09/design-extract` appear relevant to capturing an existing look, but were not read.

## Do any make measurable claims or publish before-and-after examples?

### Takeaway
Taste-skill publishes by far the most measurement, including an unflattering result: in its own blind tests by model jurors, its round score never beat the no-skill baseline (four losses and one tie). Most other repos offer side-by-side images or test harnesses without published lift numbers. No repo examined showed human-judged evidence of better output.

### Cited Findings

**Taste-skill's published test** ([research.md §11](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/main/skills/taste-skill/references/research.md))

| Round | Briefs | Skill | Baseline | Atelier | Skill wins |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 | 6.6 | 7.7 | 6.8 | 1 of 4 |
| 2 | 3 | 7.1 | 8.0 | – | 0 of 3 |
| 3 | 3 | 7.5 | 7.5 | – | 1 of 3 |
| 4 (unseen briefs) | 3 | 6.9 | 7.9 | 7.5 | 0 of 3 |
| 5 (same three as 4) | 3 | 7.7 | 7.8 | 7.2 | 2 of 3 |

- The source does not say whether round scores are means, and I read its "wins" column as the skill's wins.
- Final builds measured 0 inspector FAILs and baselines 8–13. That result is partly circular, because the inspector encodes the skill's own rules.
- Stated limits: one juror per brief per round, about half a point of noise, round 5 briefs previously seen, and jurors are models.
- The skill was revised after each round from the jurors' reasons, so scores are not independent of the test.

**Taste-skill's site studies** ([research.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/main/skills/taste-skill/references/research.md), [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/main/skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md))

- Study 1: 247 gallery sites captured on 5 October 2026, scored by 21 model reviewers using Awwwards weights. These are model judgements on still frames, not real Awwwards scores.
- Sites scoring 7.5 or higher versus under 6.5: icon feature cards 1% vs 28%, pill badges 14% vs 34%, gradients 22% vs 52%, shadows 24% vs 48%, tiny tracked caps 16% vs 57%. The weak group is small, at 23 measurable sites.
- The same study reports monospace labels, index numbers and hairlines as more common on award sites than weak ones, but the skill bans them anyway.
- The source describes the sample as reflecting fashion as much as quality.
- Study 2: 100 Awwwards Sites of the Day (10 July–17 October 2026). The median running motion was 420ms, and 42% of pages carried footage (half of the top sites, one of the 16 lowest).
- A build takes roughly 30–45 minutes and a few hundred thousand tokens — [README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/main/README.md).

**Other repos**

- Impeccable publishes one with-and-without image pair (same model and brief) and a Neo Mirai case study, with no numeric lift. It claims 62 deterministic rules — [README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pbakaus/impeccable/main/README.md).
- Hallmark has an [eval harness](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Nutlope/hallmark/main/eval/README.md) of 8 briefs with a model vision judge, sloplint scoring and conformance checks. It is repo-internal and is not shipped, and I found no published result table. Its redesign handoff format reports before and after counts per run — [redesign.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Nutlope/hallmark/main/skills/hallmark/references/redesign.md).
- ui-ux-pro-max labels its demo as not a benchmark and its before-and-after dashboard as illustrative and community-contributed, with live links — [README](https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill/blob/main/README.md). Its [relevance test fixtures](https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill/tree/main/src/ui-ux-pro-max/scripts/tests/fixtures) measure search behaviour rather than design outcomes.
- Taste-skill shows [example pages](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/main/README.md) without a paired baseline.
- Anthropic's frontend-design makes no measurable claims in its [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anthropics/skills/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md).

**Third-party side-by-side** (secondary, undated, labelled October 2026)

- One author reports frontend-design took about 3 minutes against 2.5 without it; Impeccable's `/audit` scored a page 10/20 and `/polish` took 2.5 minutes; Huashu cost about $4.20 against about $1 for others; ui-ux-pro-max gave the most conventional result — [Paralect](https://www.paralect.com/skills/top-design-skills). This is a single evaluator on a small sample, and its dial description for taste-skill is outdated.

### Inferences
- Measured benefit remains unproven across the category. The only per-round comparison found shows no round-score advantage for its skill over no skill, while the same skill does reduce its own defined tells to zero.
- The gap suggests a real risk: removing tells may not improve judged quality. Taste-skill says as much, noting that jurors preferred a richer page with tells over an empty one without them — [SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/main/skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md).
- The test section does not name the builder model, so these results may not transfer to other models.

### Gaps
- I found no human-rated, independently run, controlled evaluation of any design skill in the repos.
- "Atelier", the comparison skill in taste-skill's test, was not traced to a repo.
- [`hylarucoder/benchmark-skill-ui-ux-pro-max`](https://github.com/zhuyansen/awesome-claude-design-skills) and Impeccable's `docs/comp-fidelity-review-2026-08-17.json` may hold further data, but were not read.
- Impeccable's Neo Mirai case study and Hallmark's gallery were not opened.
